The Feedback Loop

Loop that streamlines training and improves model accuracy.

After exporting training-ready data straight into your required storage entity, in the format your framework expects. We do not stop there, we connect to your training platform, to ensure trains are streamlined and evaluates the model for any drifts. Observability layer measures prediction logs in production for any kind of drift. Data behind any degradation routes straight back to Datalier for re-labeling loop without manual intervention dimensing every drift to ensure complete model accuracy.
