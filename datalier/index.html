Clean. Validate. Prepare.
Transform

Remove duplicates, detect personally identifiable information (PII), validate field integrity, normalize LLM schema, score data quality and many more, before any labeling begins.

Explore Transform →
Annotate. Verify. Certify.
Label

A multi-engine orchestration layer that routes each data point to the appropriate AI Engine. Labels are backed by confidences and those below the threshold go to reviews before pushing it into next layer. Enriched with automation and focused on quality across all five data modalities.

Explore Label →
Snapshot. Trace. Export.
Version

Every processed AI-ready dataset is frozen into an immutable, hash-verified snapshot, with a full lineage for traceability and get AI-ready data exported in relevant form.

Explore Version →
Monitor. Detect. Correct.
Observe

Get Models registered, to pull prediction logs from deployed models back into the platform. Detect drifts to identify when and where performance is degrading, and route rectified data back through labeling pipeline back into the model training enhancing accuracy.

Explore Observe →
Control. Audit. Comply.
Govern

An immutable audit log, and regulatory tags that travel with the data wherever it moves. Ensuring trust in data binding the focus on reliability and sustainability of AI models.

Why Datalier ?

Datalier's Data layers and AI Engines are embodied to endorse the trust from ML and AI teams to fine-tune data to be training ready while accelerating the development of their AI models.

Quality

Datalier can provide the core to any data making it AI ready with high level quality metrics from AI engines and Human review system.

Cost Effective

Build lucrative AI Models training data, while being able to easily find, categorize, and fix AI model failures with our Data layers and AI engines. Then, optimize operational spend for creating high-value curated data for AI model training until attaining required level of model accuracies.
